When God Showed Up · Nov 1 + Nov 4

The Burning Bush

Text
Exodus 3:1 to 4:17
Story
Moses, 80, a shepherd after forty years of exile, sees a fire that doesn't consume. God hears his people's cry, sends Moses, and reveals his name.
Hook
Open on the bush already burning: a man who had failed and fled, now standing barefoot.
Faith talk
Moses asks "Who am I?" God answers with his presence, not Moses' résumé. For anyone who feels unqualified.
Thumbnail
HE SAW / FIRE · Exodus 3:2
Women of the Bible · Nov 8 + Nov 11

Ruth

Text
Ruth 1–4; Matthew 1:5
Story
Famine, three funerals, and a Moabite widow who refuses to leave Naomi. Gleaning in Boaz's field, a kinsman-redeemer, and a grandson in King David's line.
Hook
A foreigner, a widow and broke. God wrote her into the family of a king, and of Jesus.
Faith talk
Hesed, loyal love: commitment that stays when it costs, and God's loyal love behind it.
Thumbnail
SHE / STAYED · Ruth 1:16

Story

Weekly rhythm

One recording session a week covers that Sunday's story and the faith talk that follows it. The calendar file adds these steps as reminders.

Monday

Record narration for Sunday's story and Wednesday's faith talk. Record the next teaser Short too.

Tue–Wed

Make the Google Flow clips from the shot list. Faith talks reuse about a third of the story's clips.

Thursday

Claude builds both videos and the Shorts. Dominique reviews the renders.

Friday–Sat

Fix notes, final render, then schedule in Studio with Dominique's OK.

Before the launches

  • Create two playlists in Studio: When God Showed Up and Women of the Bible. Add each new episode and faith talk to its series playlist.
  • Give each series its own look in Flow (lighting, palette, setting). This keeps every video visibly original, which also helps with YouTube's monetization review.
  • Keep thumbnails and titles adult-facing. The Women of the Bible stories, Ruth especially, are mature stories of loss and loyalty.
  • Each publish date still needs Dominique's OK, even when the upload is scheduled.
